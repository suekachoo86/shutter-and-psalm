Who I Am

Daughter of God,
First and Foremost

Hi, I’m Sue—the digital artist and developing photographer behind Shutter & Psalm.

Before I am an artist, designer, photographer, or business owner, I am a daughter of God. That is the truest and most important thing about me.

My identity is not found in what I create, what I accomplish, or how my work is received. It is rooted in Christ—in His love, His grace, and the new life He has given me.

Shutter & Psalm flows from that identity. This work does not define me; it is one way I can serve the One who does. Everything I create begins with Him, belongs to Him, and is offered back to Him for His glory.

Creativity With a Greater Purpose

1 Peter 4:10–11  ·  Matthew 28:19

First Peter 4:10–11 reminds us that we have each been given gifts and are called to use them in service to others and for the glory of God. For me, that calling takes shape through creativity.

Shutter & Psalm was born from a desire to use my creative gifts to share the Gospel and the love, truth, and grace of Jesus Christ. Inspired by Christ’s call in Matthew 28:19 to go and make disciples, I want the things I create to do more than simply make a space beautiful. I want them to carry a message that matters.

What I Create

Art. Design. Faith.

Two creative paths, united by one purpose: to point hearts back to their Creator.

Digital Art

Scripture-inspired digital art is at the heart of what I create today. Through thoughtful design, meaningful words, and visual storytelling, I create pieces intended to bring God’s truth into homes and everyday spaces.

Photography

Photography is also becoming part of that story. As I continue developing behind the camera, I hope to capture glimpses of the beauty God has placed throughout His creation—and point that beauty back to its Creator.

My Prayer for This Work

A Message That Meets You in Every Season

My hope is that people encounter Christ through this work in every season of life—in moments of joy and celebration, as well as sorrow and pain.

I pray that each piece offers encouragement, helps someone recognize His grace and love, and inspires a deeper relationship with Him. If something I create reminds even one person that God is near, encourages them to open His Word, or helps lead their heart toward Christ, then it has fulfilled its purpose.

Help make Heaven crowded.

Ultimately, I want to use what God has given me to share His truth and reflect His beauty.
