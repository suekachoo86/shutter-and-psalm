Shutter & Psalm Creative

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Your privacy matters. Thank you for trusting Shutter & Psalm Creative with the information needed to answer your questions, share new artwork, and fulfill your orders. This page explains how information is handled when you use this website.

Information We Collect

When you interact with Shutter & Psalm Creative, the information involved depends on what you choose to do:

  • Contact requests: Your name, email address, subject, message, and any optional photos you attach when asking a question or requesting custom artwork. Please avoid including sensitive information in messages or uploads.
  • Orders: Your name, contact details, order selections, and billing, shipping, or local delivery information needed to process and fulfill a purchase.
  • Email subscriptions: Your email address, subscription status and preferences, and engagement information such as email opens or link clicks, where available.
  • Technical data: Website hosts and integrated services may process information such as IP address, device or browser details, site referrals, and basic website activity. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to inquiries; manage purchases, quotes, and customer service; deliver digital artwork; arrange production, shipping, or local pickup; send necessary order updates; and maintain business records. If you subscribe to the mailing list, it is also used to send news about artwork, shop updates, and occasional promotions. Purchasing something does not by itself subscribe you to marketing emails.

Payments and Fulfillment

Online checkout is handled through Square. This website uses a Cloudflare-hosted checkout connection to send selected items and purchase information needed to initiate checkout. Square processes payment details on its checkout service; this website does not directly store complete payment card numbers. Shutter & Psalm Creative may receive customer, transaction, and fulfillment details needed to manage an order, including through Square invoices for custom work when offered.

For canvas purchases, necessary order details and customer contact or delivery information may be provided to Printful, the production and shipping partner. For framed artwork, shipping or local pickup and delivery information is used to fulfill orders. Please see the relevant providers' own privacy notices for their practices.

Contact Forms and Email Updates

Messages and optional photo attachments submitted through the contact form are processed through Basin so I can read and respond to inquiries or custom requests. Basin may also process technical information for form delivery and abuse prevention.

Mailing-list subscriptions are managed using Kit. Kit may maintain subscription records and report email engagement, including opens and link clicks, to help me understand whether updates are useful. Signing up is voluntary; you can unsubscribe using the link in marketing emails. Purchasing artwork does not automatically enroll you in the mailing list.

When Information Is Shared

Shutter & Psalm Creative does not sell customer personal information. Information is shared with providers as reasonably needed to host and operate the website, receive messages, process purchases, fulfill products, deliver subscribed emails, prevent abuse, or comply with law. Depending on how you interact with the site, these may include GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Basin, Kit, Square, Printful, and services that host website fonts or imagery. Customer information may also be disclosed if reasonably necessary to respond to a lawful request, protect rights, or resolve a dispute.

Links to Facebook, Instagram, or other third-party websites take you to services with their own privacy policies; those links do not by themselves mean this website uses a Meta advertising pixel.

Cookies, Local Storage, and Analytics

The shop uses your browser's local storage to remember the items you add to your cart. This cart information remains in your browser until it is changed or cleared; it is not the same as a completed purchase. You can clear it through your browser settings.

The embedded Kit signup form may use cookies or similar technologies to recognize form interactions and subscription activity. Checkout and other third-party services may also use technologies necessary to provide their services.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page views, visit trends, referral sources, and website performance. According to Cloudflare, its Web Analytics service does not use visitor personal data to track individuals across websites. We have not added Google Analytics or a Meta advertising pixel to this website.

The website also uses Google-hosted fonts and certain remotely hosted images. Loading these assets can involve requests to the services that host them, which may receive technical connection information such as your IP address.

Browser settings may let you limit cookies or clear local storage, although some features may then work differently. If new advertising or tracking services are added, this notice and any required controls will be reviewed first.

Security and Retention

Shutter & Psalm Creative takes reasonable steps to protect personal information, including limiting the information requested through this website and using third-party providers for payment processing, form submissions, and fulfillment. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, such as handling an inquiry, fulfilling an order, delivering requested emails, addressing a dispute, or maintaining required business and tax records. Different service providers may retain data for different periods under their own policies. When information is no longer reasonably needed, it may be deleted or de-identified where practical and permitted by law.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails, and you may clear the saved shopping cart using your browser's storage controls.

To ask about personal information held by Shutter & Psalm Creative, request a correction, or request deletion, please use the contact page and identify the request as a privacy inquiry. I may need to verify that the requester is entitled to receive or change the information before acting. Requests will be reviewed under applicable law and responded to within any legally required timeframe. Some information may need to be retained for transactions, accounting, taxes, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Information held independently by third-party providers may also be governed by their privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. Shutter & Psalm Creative does not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If I learn that information was collected directly from a child under 13 without any required parental consent, I will take steps to delete it or otherwise address it as required by applicable law. Parents or guardians can report concerns through the contact page.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised if services, information practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top will reflect updates. If the law requires additional notice or consent for a change, those steps will be taken before the relevant change is implemented.

Contact

Shutter & Psalm Creative is based in Gainesville, Georgia, United States. For privacy questions, concerns, or information requests, use the Shutter & Psalm Creative contact form. Please include “Privacy Request” in the subject so the inquiry can be identified and addressed.
