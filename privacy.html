Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Your privacy matters. Thank you for trusting Shutter & Psalm Creative with the information needed to answer your questions, share new artwork, and fulfill your orders. This page explains how information is handled when you use this website.

Information We Collect

When you interact with Shutter & Psalm Creative, the information involved depends on what you choose to do:

  • Contact requests: Your name, email address, message, and any details you provide when asking a question or requesting custom artwork.
  • Orders: Your name, contact details, order selections, and billing, shipping, or local delivery information needed to process and fulfill a purchase.
  • Email subscriptions: Your email address and subscription-related information if you choose to join the mailing list.
  • Technical data: Website hosts and integrated services may process information such as IP address, device or browser details, and basic website activity.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to inquiries; manage purchases, quotes, and customer service; deliver digital artwork; arrange production, shipping, or local pickup; send necessary order updates; and maintain business records. If you subscribe to the mailing list, it is also used to send news about artwork, shop updates, and occasional promotions. Purchasing something does not by itself subscribe you to marketing emails.

Payments and Fulfillment

Checkout is handled through Square. Payment card information is processed by Square rather than stored directly by this website. Shutter & Psalm Creative may receive the transaction and customer details needed to manage an order. Canvas orders are produced and shipped by Printful, which receives the information needed to fulfill those purchases. These providers have their own privacy practices.

Contact Forms and Email Updates

Messages sent through the website contact form are handled using Basin. Mailing-list subscriptions are handled using Kit. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in those messages. You can also contact the business to ask about the information you submitted.

When Information Is Shared

Shutter & Psalm Creative does not sell customer personal information. Information may be shared with services needed to run the website, receive messages, process payments, deliver products, send opted-in emails, or comply with legal obligations. Depending on the interaction, those services may include GitHub Pages, Basin, Kit, Square, and Printful.

Cookies and Website Technology

This website and services embedded in or linked from it may use cookies or similar technologies for site functions, checkout, security, or service performance. Your browser settings may let you limit cookies. Details about optional analytics and tracking will be reviewed as the website is finalized before launch.

Security and Retention

Reasonable care is taken when handling customer information, including using established services for payment processing and fulfillment. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to handle requests, complete orders, maintain records, resolve problems, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. To ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by Shutter & Psalm Creative, please use the contact page. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate recordkeeping or legal reasons.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13, and Shutter & Psalm Creative does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the website, business services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date shown at the top will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled? Please reach out through the Shutter & Psalm Creative contact form.
